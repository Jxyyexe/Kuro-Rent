Kuro Rent is a boutique leasing agency based in the Greater Montreal and Laval area. We specialize in connecting qualified tenants with quality rental units — from modern studios to spacious family homes — with a level of service most agencies simply don't offer.

Whether you're relocating, upgrading, or searching for your first apartment, our team handles every detail of the leasing process: viewings, applications, screening, and signing. We work with both individual landlords and large residential buildings across Laval, LaSalle, Pointe-Claire, and downtown Montreal.

We speak your language — literally. Our team operates fully in both French and English, making the rental process smooth and stress-free for every client.

  • Bilingual service in French & English
  • Fast tenant screening & application processing
  • Individual and group viewings available
  • Landlord partnership & building contracts
  • Transparent fees — no hidden costs

What We Do

Full-Service Leasing Solutions

Apartment Leasing

From studios to large townhouses, we match tenants with the right space for their budget and lifestyle. We handle the entire process from first contact to signed lease.

Tenant Screening

We qualify every applicant thoroughly — income verification, references, credit checks — so landlords get reliable tenants and tenants get a fair process.

Building Partnerships

We partner directly with property owners and building managers to fill vacancies fast. Flexible contract structures tailored to your property and goals.
